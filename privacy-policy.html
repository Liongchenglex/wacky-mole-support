Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 12, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Wacky Mole mobile game (hereby referred to as "App") developed by Lex (hereby referred to as "Developer", "we", "us", or "our"). This App is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This privacy policy is used to inform users regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information if anyone decided to use our App.

Information Collection and Use

The App itself does not directly collect or transmit any personally identifiable information. However, the App does use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you.

Data Stored Locally

The App stores game data locally on your device, including:

  • High scores
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Game progress

This data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third parties.

Third-Party Services

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect and process certain data to provide personalized advertising and analytics. The information collected by AdMob may include:

  • Device identifiers (such as Advertising ID)
  • IP address
  • General location information
  • App usage data
  • Device and network information

For more information about how Google AdMob collects and processes data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Privacy Information

App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS devices, the App may request permission to track your activity across other apps and websites for advertising purposes. You can choose to allow or deny this permission. Your choice will affect the type of advertisements you see:

  • Allow Tracking: You may see personalized ads based on your interests and activity
  • Deny Tracking: You will see non-personalized ads that are less relevant to your interests

You can change your tracking preference at any time in your device Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children. The App is intended for general audiences and does not target children specifically. However, we recognize that children may use the App. We rely on AdMob's compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and other applicable laws regarding children's privacy.

If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has used the App, please contact us so that we can take necessary actions.

Permissions

The App requires the following permissions:

  • Internet Access: Required to display advertisements through Google AdMob
  • Network State: Used to check network connectivity for ad delivery

No other permissions are requested or required by the App.

Data Security

We value your trust in using our App. Since we do not collect or store personal information on our servers, there is no user data transmitted or stored by us. However, please be aware that data transmitted over the internet to third-party services (such as AdMob) is subject to the security practices of those services.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new privacy policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.

You are advised to review this privacy policy periodically for any changes. Changes to this privacy policy are effective when they are posted on this page.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your data:

  • The right to access information collected about you
  • The right to request deletion of your data
  • The right to opt-out of personalized advertising

Since the App does not collect personal information directly, most of these rights would apply to data collected by third-party services like AdMob. Please refer to Google's privacy controls and settings to exercise these rights.
